# ADR-0507 — OTel Stretch Confined to Execution Service Only

**Status:** ACCEPTED
**Date:** 2026-05-20
**Reversal cost:** LOW
**Owner:** Principal Engineering

---

## Context

OpenTelemetry tracing is a P2/ChaosForge stretch goal. The question is which service(s) carry the experiment and what the scope boundary is.

---

## Decision

OTel adoption is **confined to exactly one service: the Execution Service**. No other ChaosForge service (Gateway, Control Plane) adopts OTel. P1 baseline (Micrometer + Brave/Zipkin) remains unchanged.

Permitted in the stretch: `opentelemetry-spring-boot-starter` on the Execution Service; dual-export of metrics during a controlled experiment; OTLP trace emission to the local Compose Tempo instance. Forbidden: other services adopting OTel; removal of Brave/Zipkin from the experimental service (dual-export is the contract, not replacement).

If the experiment produces a finding that justifies workspace-wide adoption, a new **P1 ADR** is required — registered in `[P1]/arch-decisions-log.md`, not here.

---

## Alternatives

**OTel on all three services simultaneously:** Increases experiment surface and makes it harder to isolate OTel-specific failures from application failures during the stretch weeks. Rejected.

**OTel on Gateway (highest request volume):** Gateway is the free-tier deployed service; OTel collector is not reachable there (ADR-0505). Rejected.

---

## Consequences

**Positive:** Experiment is bounded and reversible. If OTel causes issues in the Execution Service, only one service rolls back.

**Negative:** Trace correlation across all three services requires the Execution Service to propagate W3C TraceContext headers to its own outbound calls; incoming traces from CP (Brave/Zipkin) need a bridge propagator. Coexistence risk documented in Known Limitations.

---

## Failure Modes

| Failure | Detection | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Brave + OTel span correlation breaks under load | Trace IDs don't match across CP→Exec boundary | Fall back to Brave-only on Exec; record ADR-0522 |
